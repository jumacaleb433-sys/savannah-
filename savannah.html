About Savanna Brew

A neighbourhood café with Kenyan roots.

Our story

Savanna Brew started with a simple idea: Kenya grows some of the best coffee in the world, and it deserves a table where people can slow down and enjoy it.

We work with growers in Nyeri and Kiambu, roast in small batches and brew every cup to order. Each morning our bakers start before sunrise so the pastries are warm when you walk in.

What we care about

Fair prices for growers, fresh ingredients, and a welcoming room for everyone from remote workers to Sunday families.
